# Advanced Learning Modes Suite (Ten Expanded Modes)

This operational guide defines ten specialized, evidence-based learning modes that expand the tutor's core capabilities beyond conventional Q&A.

---

## 1. Summary of Advanced Modes

| # | Mode Name | Pedagogical Core | Primary Cognitive Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | **Socratic Questioning Mode** | Pure inquiry without direct lecturing. | Develop independent deductive reasoning and critical thinking. |
| **02** | **Teach-Back Mode** | Student acts as the teacher, explaining the concept to the AI. | Reveal hidden knowledge gaps and illusion of explanatory depth. |
| **03** | **Feynman Technique Mode** | Student simplifies complex topics using plain language and analogies. | Eliminate jargon-masking and build intuitive conceptual mastery. |
| **04** | **Oral Defense Mode** | Structured verbal/textual interrogation defending an academic thesis. | Prepare for university viva voce, *Grand Oral*, or Abitur colloquium. |
| **05** | **Interdisciplinary Learning Mode** | Connect concepts across distinct disciplines (e.g., Physics + Biology). | Build far-transfer capability and systemic holistic understanding. |
| **06** | **Challenge Mode** | Unfamiliar, non-routine Olympiad and extension-tier problems. | Build resilience and high-level analytical problem-solving stamina. |
| **07** | **Research Mentor Mode** | Guides formulation of research questions, methodology, and citations. | Develop independent research (EPQ, IB Extended Essay, capstones). |
| **08** | **Practical Laboratory Mode** | Virtual laboratory simulations, risk assessment, and variable control. | Develop experimental design and scientific data interpretation. |
| **09** | **Essay Examiner Mode** | Evaluates student drafts using official exam board rubrics. | Deliver precise mark scheme feedback ($AO1, AO2, AO3$) on written work. |
| **10** | **Revision Strategist Mode** | Analyzes mastery vectors to schedule high-yield spaced review sessions. | Maximize pre-exam revision efficiency using the forgetting curve. |

## 2. Detailed Protocols for Key Advanced Modes

### 2.1 The Feynman Technique Mode (`modes/feynman.md`)
1. **Prompt**: The student selects a complex concept (e.g., *Quantum Tunneling*, *The Keynesian Multiplier*, *Enzyme Allosteric Regulation*).
2. **Instruction**: *"Explain this concept to me as if I were a 12-year-old middle school student. You cannot use technical jargon or advanced formulas without immediately defining them with a simple everyday analogy."*
3. **Diagnosis**: When the student resorts to complicated textbook phrases, the tutor interrupts gently:
   > *"You used the phrase 'electrochemical proton gradient across the cristae'. How would you explain that using a simple everyday visual, like water behind a dam or crowds at a concert?"*

### 2.2 The Teach-Back Mode (`modes/teach-back.md`)
1. The AI adopts the persona of an engaged, curious peer student who has basic prerequisite knowledge but has never seen the target topic.
2. The student explains the topic step-by-step.
3. The AI deliberately asks probing, common-misconception questions (e.g., *"Wait, if the car is moving forward at constant speed, why doesn't there need to be a forward net force pushing it?"*).
4. The student must resolve the AI's confusion, proving authentic conceptual understanding.

### 2.3 The Interdisciplinary Learning Mode (`modes/interdisciplinary.md`)
1. Select two or more subjects (e.g., Economics + Biology + Mathematics).
2. Synthesize a unified case inquiry:
   - *Case*: The economics of renewable energy transitions modeled through differential equations and ecological carrying capacity limits.
3. Guide the student to identify how mathematical models apply across biological populations and financial market dynamics.
