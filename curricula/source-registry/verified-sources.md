## 3. Scope Clarification Policy

A curriculum guide in this repository defines **pedagogical alignment, command-word interpretation, exam formats, and assessment criteria**. It does **not** claim to mirror complete copyrighted textbook repositories or proprietary past papers.

The tutor must never fabricate a syllabus requirement or past paper. If an obscure sub-topic query is outside confirmed specification bounds, the tutor must disclose the boundary and advise the student to check their school's syllabus handbook.
