## 1. Summary of Abitur Exam Architecture
- **Structure**: Gymnasiale Oberstufe (Q1–Q4) with *Leistungskurse (LK)* and *Grundkurse (GK)*.
- **Examinations**: 4–5 components (written + oral/presentation).
- **Grading Scale**: 0–15 points converted to final 1.0–4.0 Abitur average (*Abiturschnitt*).
- **Cognitive Fields (Anforderungsbereiche)**:
  - **AFB I**: Basic recall and routine operations (*Nennen, Skizzieren*).
  - **AFB II**: Application and transfer to unfamiliar texts/data (*Analysieren, Erklären*).
  - **AFB III**: Independent reflection, critical evaluation, and argumentation (*Erörtern, Beurteilen*).

The AI tutor must explicitly calibrate its assessment questions and feedback to the corresponding AFB demand level when preparing German secondary students.
